# Mentorship Guide: Holistic Academic & Professional Skill Intelligence

## Problem Overview
A "living" platform that tracks learning journeys and aligns them with industry needs in Health-Tech, Agri-Tech, and Smart Cities.

## 1. Solution Approaches & Architectures

### A. Graph-Based Skill Mapping
*   **Why**: Skills are interconnected. Learning "Data Science" is relevant to both Healthcare (Bioinformatics) and Smart Cities (Traffic Flow).
*   **Tech**: Neo4j or GraphDB to map "User -> knows -> Skill" and "Job -> requires -> Skill".

### B. Evidence-Based Portfolios (Blockchain/Verifiable)
*   Storing certificates and project outcomes as Verifiable Credentials (linking back to Problem 2) to prevent resume fraud.

### C. AI-Driven Gap Analysis & Recommendations
*   A recommendation engine (Collaborative Filtering or Content-Based) that suggests courses based on the user's current level and target industry.

## 2. Advanced Concepts to Look For
*   **NLP for Skill Extraction**: Using AI to parse project reports and resumes to "extract" skills automatically.
*   **Knowledge Graphs**: Building a hierarchy of skills (e.g., "React" is a child of "Frontend" which is a child of "Web Dev").
*   **Transferable Skills**: Identifying how a skill in one domain (e.g., IoT in Smart Cities) applies to another (e.g., Precision Farming).

## 3. Mentorship & Judging Questions

### Mentorship (To challenge the team)
*   "How do you measure 'Practical Skills' vs. just 'Theoretical Knowledge' (certificates)?"
*   "How do you prevent 'Skill Inflation'? Just because someone watched a video doesn't mean they are an expert."
*   "How do you handle 'Cold Start'? If a new student joins with zero history, how do you give them a path?"

### Judging (To evaluate the solution)
*   "How does your 'Intelligence' part work? Is it just a dashboard, or does it actually predict future career success?"
*   "How do you ensure data privacy for students? Can an employer see a student's 'failed' projects?"
*   "Is the system adaptable? If a new technology arrives (e.g., Quantum Computing in Health), how quickly can your platform include it?"

## 4. Judging Criteria
*   **Actionable Insights**: Does the system give clear "Next Steps" to the user?
*   **Data Integrity**: How do you verify that someone actually did the project they claimed?
*   **UI/UX**: Is the "Learning Path" visualization intuitive or just a list of items?
